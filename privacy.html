TimestampCamera ("the App", "we", "us") is developed by Kuwat Subhi. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1.Information We Access

The App requests the following device permissions to provide its core functionality:

Permission Purpose
Camera To capture timestamped photos and videos.
Location (fine/coarse) To embed date, time, and location information onto photos and videos you capture.
Microphone To record audio when you capture a video.
Internet / Network state To upload photos and videos to your own Google Drive when you choose to do so.

All photos, videos, and location data are generated and processed locally on your device. We do not operate our own servers and we do not collect, receive, or store any of this content ourselves.

2.Google Drive Access (drive.file scope)

TimestampCamera offers an optional feature to back up captured photos and videos to your Google Drive account. To do this, the App requests Google authorization using the restricted https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope.

This scope means:

  • The App can only access files that it itself creates or that you explicitly open with the App through Google Drive's file picker.
  • The App cannot view, browse, or modify any other files already in your Google Drive.
  • Files are uploaded directly from your device to your own Google Drive account, using Google's servers. TimestampCamera does not have its own backend server and never receives a copy of your files.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, data obtained through the Google Drive API is used solely to let you save and view your own timestamped photos/videos in your own Drive. It is:

  • Not used for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • Not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • Not used to train or improve generalized AI/ML models.
  • Not read, transferred, or disclosed to anyone except as necessary to provide the backup feature you requested, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with continued protection under this policy).
  • Only accessed by automated systems (no human review), except where necessary for security purposes, to comply with applicable law, or with your explicit consent.

3.Data Storage & Retention

  • Photos and videos captured with the App are stored on your device's local storage until you delete them or the App's cleanup routine removes locally cached copies (only after a successful upload, if you enable Drive backup).
  • If you enable Google Drive backup, uploaded files are stored in your own Google Drive and are subject to Google's own privacy and retention policies.
  • We do not maintain any external database or server containing your photos, videos, or location data.

4.Your Choices & Control

  • Camera, location, and microphone permissions can be granted or revoked at any time through your device's Settings.
  • Google Drive access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Revoking access does not delete files already uploaded to your Drive.
  • You may delete locally stored photos/videos directly within the App or via your device's file manager.

5.Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

About the App

TimestampCamera lets you capture photos and videos with the date, time, and location stamped directly onto the media — useful for documentation, fieldwork, inspections, or any situation where proof of when and where a photo was taken matters.

Features

  • Timestamp and location overlay on photos and videos
  • Local gallery of your captures
  • Optional backup of your photos/videos to your own Google Drive

Permissions Used

  • Camera — to take photos and record video
  • Location — to stamp coordinates onto your media
  • Microphone — to record audio in videos
  • Internet — to upload backups to your own Google Drive (optional)

See the full Privacy Policy for details on how each permission is used.
